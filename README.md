# Land Invaders

**Land Invaders** is an experimental 8-bit video game inspired by the look and feel of vintage arcade classics. It imagines an alternative reality in which Columbus’ ships, the _Niña_, the _Pinta_, and the _Santa Maria_, were vanquished before ever reaching the shores of Turtle Island.

Created by **Cassandra Gardiner** and **Juan Mateo Menendez** of the design studio **Bird x Bird**, the project blends retro gameplay mechanics with speculative history and personal reflection.

---

## About the Project

While grounded in the visual language and mechanics of early arcade games, _Land Invaders_ is not nostalgia for nostalgia’s sake. During development, the game became a cathartic and symbolic space: a way to confront and reframe historical forces that have inflicted lasting harm on Indigenous and marginalized communities.

By placing the player in a position of agency, the project explores how gameplay can function as a form of healing. In this sense, _Land Invaders_ treats play as a way to address intergenerational trauma and, metaphorically, to reprogram new neural pathways through interaction, repetition, and reimagining outcomes.

---

## How to Play

_Land Invaders_ follows the structure of classic arcade shooters. Controls are intentionally minimal, echoing the constraints of early arcade games.

The player controls a defender positioned at the bottom of the screen. Enemy ships descend from above in coordinated formations. The objective is to survive, defend the land, and prevent the invaders from reaching the bottom of the screen.

---

## Controls

### Keyboard

- **Left Arrow / A** — Move left
- **Right Arrow / D** — Move right
- **Spacebar** — Fire
- **Enter / Space** — Start game
- **Esc** — Quit or return to menu
